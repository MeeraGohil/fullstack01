Our Story

More Than Just Food

Encave Café was born on the MIT WPU campus with a single belief — that students deserve more than canteen food. We wanted a place that felt like a warm escape from lectures, deadlines, and late-night study sessions.

Today, Encave is spread across 6 locations on campus, serving everything from morning poha to late-evening Maggi. We cook fresh, we keep it affordable, and we make it fast — because we know you've got a 9 AM class.
